Firmware/source contract

The local Iris gen2 source declares 96x96 minima in platform_inst_cap_sm8550;
320x240 is above those advertised limits. That checkout is not proved identical
to the deployment kernel and cannot explain a deployment failure by itself.
iris_hfi_gen2_response.c classifies 0x04000003 as HFI_ERROR_FATAL, separately
from HFI_ERROR_STREAM_UNSUPPORTED, and marks both queues/session erroneous.
A fatal session event supplies no offending command/property in that report.
Therefore a fatal log alone cannot establish unsupported B-frames, an SPS error,
or a particular HFI buffer-allocation defect. Historical native failures also
prevent claiming the VA translator is uniquely responsible, but history does
not replace a fresh exact-binary reproduction.

Concrete source-level lead (hypothesis, not an implemented fix)

/home/mq/oss/linux/drivers/media/platform/qcom/iris/iris_hfi_gen2_response.c
maps either HFI_BUF_FW_FLAG_LAST or HFI_BUF_FW_FLAG_PSC_LAST to V4L2 LAST.
Its output-buffer completion function (around394) marks a zero-sized buffer
ERROR while STREAMING unless HFI_BUF_FW_FLAG_LAST is set; PSC_LAST is not
exempted there. Thus a zero-byte PSC_LAST can become LAST|ERROR even when it is
an intentional picture-sequence-change marker, without corrupt/overflow flags.
iris_buffer.c (around767) then handles ERROR by clearing payload/timestamp and
returning before its LAST/EOS/v4l2_m2m stopped bookkeeping. This asymmetry is a
specific candidate for inconsistent PSC completion state. It also fits the
native all-zero corrupt-frame symptom: local FFmpeg returns an AVFrame when
V4L2 ERROR is present instead of treating an empty marker as decoded pixels.
However, no matching-running-kernel source/build identity or raw HFI marker
trace is available in this lane. The first LAST|ERROR could alternatively be
actual firmware data_corrupt/NOSHOW/overflow or error-buffer return. The fatal
may precede that buffer rather than result from START. Do not call the source
asymmetry the proved cause, and do not apply a kernel patch from this checkout.
