Source fixes
------------
* vaBeginPicture waits once, with the existing bounded sync, for a same-context
  Pending surface before reuse. It releases the mutex during sync and then
  revalidates handles/state. InProgress and cross-context ownership still fail.
  A seek otherwise loses its new IDR when the client reuses an old surface.
* V4L2 ownership timestamps are unique submission cookies, independent of media
  POC. Replays retain their original cookies; allocation fails before wrap.
* An IDR finishes previous owners before replacing the GOP. A short bounded
  completion wait precedes STOP to avoid resetting ordinary pipeline tails.
* Only after dequeuing LAST and returning all OUTPUT buffers, owners that
  firmware discarded become explicit failed completions. They receive no
  successful pixels; publication marks them Dead and preserves export lifetime.
* SOURCE_CHANGE resume cycles an already-streaming CAPTURE queue for CPU-copy
  sessions too. The kernel's streamon path restores input-internal buffers and
  STAGE/PIPE; bare START left the one-frame session unable to consume OUTPUT.
* mpv startup accepts only the typed transient property-unavailable response
  within its original bounded wait. Post-seek hardware-property loss still fails.
* H.264 synthesis now reads the bounded, unescaped slice prefix and preserves
  its PPS identity, which VA's parsed fields omit. Every slice in a picture
  must agree, and truncated/invalid prefixes fail. Synthesized PPS points to
  the driver's SPS0; the compressed slices retain their original identity.
  IDR recognition accepts reference headers25/45/65 and rejects nonreference05.
* Concurrent CPU working-pool bounds exposed top-up assumptions in submission,
  pre-IDR completion waits, drain waits and replay. These now replenish the
  whole unreserved working pool for streaming CPU sessions too, preserve
  foreign/exported reservations, and latch replay top-up failures.
