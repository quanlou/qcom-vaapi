Sustained-duration limitation
At 30 FPS the default 3000-frame leg takes about 100 seconds; at 50 FPS it
takes about 60 seconds. Faster completion fails the minimum-duration check
honestly. A second run can use a fresh results directory and increase loops
based on the observed speed, up to the existing 100-loop bound. If throughput
exceeds 100 FPS, even 6000 frames cannot sustain 60 seconds: a longer fixture
or an explicitly reviewed harness change is required. Each leg also has a
120-second timeout; a long/slower loop choice may timeout. Do not remove the
minimum-duration threshold to claim sustained qualification. The current
measurement includes decode, CPU download and checksum, not display/render
throughput, and peak process RSS does not establish long-term leak freedom.
