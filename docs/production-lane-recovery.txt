Recovery production lane — 2026-10-01

Implemented (host validated, not hardware qualified)

* Replaced the 64-access-unit sliding suffix with complete-GOP retention:
  maximum 1,024 access units and 32 MiB of compressed bytes per session.
  A keyframe starts the chain. Exceeding either limit, allocation failure,
  or a duplicate timestamp clears the complete chain. Further inter pictures
  do not rebuild a suffix; only another bounded keyframe restores eligibility.
  Normal decoding can continue without recovery history; a subsequent transition
  requiring unavailable references fails explicitly.
* Published-timestamp proof retains 1,024 entries to match the history cap.
* Drain replay permits at most 2,500 pump(2 ms) iterations across the entire
  GOP, rather than a separate budget per picture. The nominal requested wait
  budget is five seconds; ioctl/processing overhead adds wall time. Work is
  additionally bounded by the 1,024-access-unit cap. START followed by failed
  or aborted replay latches abandonment, and submission rejects abandonment
  even when the aborted flag is unset. Replay temporarily duplicates the
  retained compressed history (up to another 32 MiB).
* Rebuild no longer truncates FIFO owners to fit queued OUTPUT. A consumed
  input can still own an unpublished VA surface; dropping it would strand
  the surface after an apparently successful rebuild.
* Drain resume pumps existing FIFO owners, hidden-waiting owners, and queued
  OUTPUT to completion before START, including when a new keyframe arrives.
  Waiting is bounded by 2,500 pump iterations and a five-second wall deadline.
  Completed surfaces remain queued for normal publication. A budget expiry
  returns a retryable session error without START, abandonment, or owner loss.
  Keyframe submission no longer clears hidden owners, and newly submitted
  headers are stored only after successful drain resume. Hidden CAPTURE markers
  cannot safely be attributed to ownerless replay while old owners remain.
  A trailing hidden reference without publication/completion proof still fails
  closed instead of silently omitting that dependency.

Compatibility limit and remaining implementation blocker

The consumed-reference rebuild guard remains. Complete history alone cannot
safely restore references in the current completion routing. Display CAPTURE
uses timestamp-to-FIFO ownership, but empty hidden CAPTURE retires FIFO.first()
without an independently verified replay owner/epoch. Feeding consumed hidden
references while current owners are present can retire an unrelated pending
surface. A correct implementation needs explicit replay-epoch ownership and
completion evidence for every hidden access unit, suppression of duplicate
published output, and preservation of pending surface/export identity across
new pools. Do not replace the guard with synthesized SPS/PPS or a queued tail.

STOP acceptance does not imply drain completion. Before START, existing owners
are pumped within the bounded wait above. If delayed owners cannot finish in
that budget, the session stays healthy and draining, so later completion and
resubmission remain possible. A fresh keyframe bypasses old references but does
not permit dropping outstanding owners. The submit API currently returns only
Result<(), ()>: budget expiry maps to VA decoding error and marks the new picture
Dead, rather than reporting VA_BUSY. Preserving VA-level retry of that same
picture requires a typed submit failure and matching decode callback changes.
Host tests cover pending visible/hidden owners and real keyframe submission
with hidden owner [7], preserved headers, no queued input, and a later retry;
only hardware can establish successful asynchronous completion timing. Existing
long-GOP pixel correctness must be rerun on the decoder host.
GOPs above either budget remain explicitly unrecoverable until the next keyframe.
